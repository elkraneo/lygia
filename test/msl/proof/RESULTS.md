| LYGIA | Optimization | 1 octave | 8 octaves |

| before (origin/main) | -O0 | doesn't link | doesn't link |

#### barycentric

"before" is the Metal port before the fix (the triangle module isn't on upstream main). For the point (0.2, 0.3) in the triangle (0,0), (1,0), (0,1), [0] is the weights and their sum, [1] is the point rebuilt from them.
